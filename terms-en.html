Booking and payment terms

Last updated: 24 September 2026. These terms apply to transfer bookings arranged by WhatsApp, email or telephone.

Quotation and availability

Each transfer is quoted according to the route, date, time, passengers, luggage and other information provided by the client. Availability and the final price are subject to written confirmation by Traslados Buenos Aires.

Required booking deposit

A deposit equal to at least 20% of the total quoted price is required to confirm and hold a booking. A booking is not confirmed until the deposit has cleared. The deposit is credited towards the total service price; the balance is paid by the agreed method before or at the start of the transfer. Paying the full amount at the destination is only possible when expressly agreed in writing and does not replace the booking deposit.

Payment methods

Accepted methods are cash in Argentine pesos, converted at the official Banco Nación exchange rate in force when the quotation is issued; physical United States dollars; United States dollar bank transfer; USDT exclusively on the TRC20 network; and credit cards through a bank POS terminal. Sending, receiving and intermediary-bank fees are the client’s responsibility. Payment is confirmed once funds have cleared.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

Requests must be sent by WhatsApp or email. The notice time is the date and time at which Traslados Buenos Aires receives the message, expressed in Argentina official time.

Cancellation before the service day

  • 72 hours or more: 100% refund of the deposit, less only non-recoverable bank or payment-platform fees.
  • Between 24 and 72 hours: 50% refund of the deposit or penalty-free rescheduling within the following 6 months, subject to availability.
  • Less than 24 hours: the deposit is non-refundable.

Missed, cancelled or rescheduled flight on the service day

For airport-arrival transfers, the flight number is required to confirm the booking. Ordinary schedule changes may be monitored with that information; if the passenger misses the flight, it is cancelled, the itinerary changes, or the passenger does not arrive on the stated flight, the passenger must notify us by WhatsApp or email within the following periods:

  • Flights from Latin America lasting 2 to 4 hours: within 30 minutes after the original flight scheduled departure time.
  • Flights from Europe, the United States or Asia lasting more than 8 hours: at least 4 hours before the scheduled arrival time in Buenos Aires.

If notice is received within the applicable period, the booking will be rescheduled for the new flight at no charge, subject to availability. If the new time cannot be covered, 100% of the deposit will be refunded, less the non-recoverable fees stated above. If notice is not received in time and the vehicle has already travelled towards the airport, the deposit is non-refundable because the vehicle was reserved and operational costs were incurred.

Airport arrival and meeting

To confirm an arrival transfer, we request the airline and flight number, number of passengers, approximate number and size of luggage items, arrival airport, and hotel or final destination. We do not request a room number. After immigration, baggage collection and customs, the passenger must message the assigned driver via WhatsApp; the driver will provide the precise meeting point and assist the passenger to the vehicle.

Airport waiting time and departure

Courtesy allowance

Once the passenger tells the assigned driver by WhatsApp that immigration, baggage collection and customs are complete and that the passenger is ready for collection, up to 10 minutes of waiting at the stated meeting point are included at no additional charge.

Additional waiting time

If boarding exceeds those 10 minutes for reasons unrelated to the prior arrangement, additional waiting time begins to accrue. The rate is US$15 for each additional 30 minutes of waiting, equivalent to US$30 per hour. When the passenger boards, the driver will transparently state the accumulated additional waiting time and the applicable charge before the journey begins. To avoid additional charges, the passenger should contact the driver only after collecting luggage and heading to the meeting or pickup area.

Invoicing

Electronic invoices are issued directly by the service provider and may be sent by email or WhatsApp. Clients may request an invoice and must provide the tax details needed to issue it.
