Data, purpose and payments

This website uses no analytics, behavioural advertising, tracking pixels or first-party cookies. It does not save quote information on its servers. If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, telephone or email, you choose to provide the information needed to request and coordinate a transfer, such as origin, destination, date, passengers, luggage and contact details.

Booking and payment are arranged outside this website. Accepted methods are cash in Argentine pesos —converted at the official Banco Nación exchange rate in force when the quotation is issued—, physical United States dollars, United States dollar bank transfer to an account at Banco Santander Argentina or Banco Macro, USDT exclusively on the TRC20 network, and credit cards. The client may choose the available payment method they prefer when arranging the booking. Bank details are shared only after the booking is confirmed; any sending, receiving or intermediary-bank fees charged on a transfer are the responsibility of the client, and payment is confirmed once funds have cleared. Card payments are handled through a bank POS terminal. This website does not request, process or store card numbers, passwords, bank details or payment credentials.

Electronic invoicing

Invoices are issued directly by the service provider and may be sent to the client by email or WhatsApp. Only the tax and contact details needed to issue the invoice are used. Payment providers process data under their own terms, security measures and privacy policies.

Minimum data for coordination

To quote and coordinate a transfer, we request only name, date and time, origin and destination, flight number where applicable, number of passengers and luggage, and a contact telephone number. We do not request through this website, WhatsApp or email photographs or numbers of identity or travel documents, card details, security codes, passwords, client bank details or sensitive personal data. If an exceptional legal obligation requires additional information, we will explain its purpose and the sending channel in advance.

Legal basis and retention

Where the GDPR applies, enquiries and bookings are processed to take steps at your request before entering into a contract and, when a service is confirmed, to perform that contract. Enquiries that do not result in a booking are kept for up to 90 days from the last contact and are then deleted or anonymised. Once a service is confirmed, data is kept only as long as needed to provide the journey, meet legal obligations or resolve claims. It is not used for marketing without a valid legal basis.

Recipients and transfers

When you open WhatsApp, the conversation is processed by WhatsApp/Meta under its own terms and policies, which may involve international transfers. This website does not load that service automatically; the link opens only when you select it. Technical hosting is provided by Vercel; this website does not send conversation data to Vercel. Email is hosted with Hostinger and managed through Gmail; Hostinger, Gmail and, where the client chooses to pay by card, the bank terminal may process only the data needed for their respective services.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection to processing, and portability where applicable. You may also lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. Send requests to the contact email above.

Security and cache
